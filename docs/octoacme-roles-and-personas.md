# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads design and execute comprehensive testing strategies to ensure quality meets acceptance criteria. They collaborate with developers and product managers to validate features before release.

### Responsibilities
- Create test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Design and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end tests
- Report defects and verify fixes
- Participate in Definition of Done discussions
- Advocate for quality standards and testing best practices

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards before release
- Minimize production defects and rework
- Enable fast feedback loops for developers

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and review meetings
- Test case documentation and defect reports
- Collaboration with developers on test coverage

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to define testability requirements and validate implementations
- Aligns with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Supports **Project Managers** by providing quality assessments for release readiness

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects guide the high-level design and technical strategy for projects. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Guide technology selections and trade-offs
- Ensure alignment with organizational standards and best practices
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable long-term scalability and performance

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical discussions
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technical guidance in planning and execution phases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with **Developers** to establish design patterns and provide technical mentorship
- Advises **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and scalability implications
- Collaborates with **Project Managers** on technical risk assessment and timeline impact

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Release Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and release processes. They enable safe, reliable deployments and maintain system observability.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure
- Manage deployment processes and rollback procedures
- Ensure security scanning and compliance in the release process
- Monitor post-deployment health and alerting
- Document and improve release procedures

### Goals
- Enable frequent, safe deployments to production
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and rollbacks
- Maintain high system reliability and observability

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and pre-deployment reviews
- Incident response and post-incident reviews
- Documentation of deployment procedures and runbooks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with **Developers** on deployment pipelines and CI/CD best practices
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on release scheduling and deployment windows
- Partners with **QA/Testing Leads** on smoke testing and deployment validation

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors are senior leaders who champion the project, secure resources, and ensure alignment with business strategy. They provide final decision authority on go/no-go decisions and prioritization trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Secure budget, resources, and organizational support
- Make executive-level decisions at key gates (initiation, planning, release)
- Communicate project importance to broader organization
- Address escalations and organizational dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maximize ROI and strategic alignment
- Remove organizational barriers to success

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Escalation and decision gates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Empowers **Project Managers** with resources and decision authority at critical gates
- Aligns **Product Managers** on business priorities and strategic direction
- Advocates for the project across organizational boundaries to unblock **Developers** and cross-functional teams
